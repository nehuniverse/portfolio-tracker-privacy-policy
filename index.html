Privacy Policy for Portfolio Tracker

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Portfolio Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") handles user data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Our application is designed with user privacy in mind:

  • Financial Data: All asset and portfolio data entered by the user is stored locally on your device using local storage (`shared_preferences`). We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal financial data on external servers.
  • Personal Information: We do not require account registration, login, or any personally identifiable information (PII) to use the app.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain device information (such as Advertising ID, IP address, and device model) to serve relevant ads and perform analytics in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.

For more information on how Google handles advertising data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.
